Three ways to play

Play your way

Solo against the bot, side-by-side on one device, or against a friend anywhere.

Single Player

Go head-to-head with the built-in AI. It completes patterns the moment they're open and blocks your best moves — good practice before you take on a friend.

Rules in a minute

How to play

Pick a piece

Choose an oval or a triangle from your cup. Change your mind? Tap it again to drop it back before placing.

Place it

Tap an open slot on the Flower of Life that matches your piece. Player 1 starts on the white pieces, Player 2 on black.

Score or block

Complete one of the nine secret symbols to score, or place strategically to keep your opponent from completing theirs.

The game ends when neither player can complete any more symbols. Highest score wins. Keep practicing against the computer and your friends, and eventually you'll become a Master of Da Vinci's Challenge.
